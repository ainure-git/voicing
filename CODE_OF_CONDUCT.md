# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the
Voicing project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and welcoming.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments,
personal or political attacks, and publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers
via a private GitHub Security Advisory or a direct message to the repository
owner. Maintainers will review and respond in a way they deem appropriate, and
will respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
